## Detailed Description

**Surfer: Your Browsing Companion**

Surfer is a fun Chrome extension that adds a video overlay to any webpage you're browsing. Watch entertaining Subway Surfers or Minecraft Parkour gameplay while reading articles, shopping online, or doing research!

**Key Features:**

• **Video Overlay**: Adds a YouTube video overlay to any web page
• **Multiple Video Options**: Choose between popular Subway Surfers or Minecraft Parkour videos
• **Smart Positioning**: Video automatically moves to the opposite side of the screen when hovered over
• **Non-intrusive**: Video doesn't interfere with your browsing - no controls or interactions to distract you
• **Persistent Settings**: Extension remembers your video selection between browsing sessions

**How to Use:**

1. Click the Surfer icon in your Chrome toolbar
2. Select your preferred video from the dropdown menu
3. Click "Toggle Video" to show or hide the video overlay
4. When you hover over the video, it automatically moves to the opposite side of the screen
5. Click anywhere on the page to make the video move out of your way

**Perfect For:**

• Students who need background entertainment while studying
• Professionals who enjoy ambient videos while working
• Anyone who wants to multitask while browsing the web

Surfer is lightweight, secure, and respects your privacy. We don't collect any personal data, and all preferences are stored locally on your device.

Enhance your browsing experience today with Surfer!

# Privacy Policy for Surfer Chrome Extension

## Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how the Surfer Chrome Extension ("we", "our", or "extension") collects, uses, and discloses information about you. By using our extension, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

## Information Collection and Use

The Surfer Chrome Extension does not collect any personal information from its users. We do not:
- Track your browsing history
- Collect your personal data
- Share any information with third parties
- Use cookies or similar tracking technologies

## Data Storage

The extension uses Chrome's storage API (`chrome.storage.local`) to store:
- Your video selection preference
- The active state of the extension

This data is stored locally on your device and is not transmitted to any external servers.

## Permissions

Our extension requires the following permissions:
- `activeTab`: To add the video overlay to the current tab
- `storage`: To save your preferences locally
- `scripting`: To inject content scripts into web pages
- `host_permissions` for `<all_urls>`: To allow the extension to function on all websites

These permissions are used solely for the functionality of the extension and not for data collection.

## Third-Party Services

The extension embeds YouTube videos. When you use this feature, YouTube's privacy policy applies to the video content. We recommend reviewing [YouTube's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) for more information.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on the Chrome Web Store listing. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.
